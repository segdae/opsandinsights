# Engagement Playbook — How to Use the Dream 30

## The 30-Day Activation Plan

### Week 1: Foundation
- [ ] Follow all 30 people on LinkedIn
- [ ] Turn on notifications for all 10 Tier 1 creators
- [ ] Create a LinkedIn bookmark folder or Notion tracker for their posts
- [ ] Read their last 10 posts each — note hooks, formats, CTAs

### Week 2: Visibility
- [ ] Comment on 3-5 Tier 1 posts per day (thoughtful, value-adding comments)
- [ ] Comment on 1-2 Tier 2 posts per day
- [ ] Share 1 post from the Dream 30 with your own perspective added
- [ ] Begin noting their content patterns (posting times, formats, topics)

### Week 3: Value Creation
- [ ] Reference a Dream 30 creator in one of your own posts (tag them)
- [ ] Reply to their comments on other people's posts too (expand the surface area)
- [ ] Start DM conversations with 2-3 Tier 1 creators (genuine, no asks)
- [ ] Create a "What I learned from [Creator]" style post

### Week 4: Relationship Building
- [ ] Pitch a content collaboration to 1-2 creators (podcast swap, co-post, template review)
- [ ] Write a post mentioning 3-5 of them as recommended follows (they'll notice)
- [ ] Continue daily commenting cadence
- [ ] Assess: which creators engage back? Double down on those relationships.

## Comment Strategy

### The 3-Layer Comment Framework

**Layer 1 — Validate** (1 sentence)
Acknowledge their insight. Show you actually read the post.

**Layer 2 — Add** (1-2 sentences)
Share a related experience, data point, or contrarian angle from your own work.

**Layer 3 — Extend** (1 sentence)
Ask a question or make a prediction that invites further discussion.

### Example Comments by Tier

**On a Ruben Hassid post about AI content workflows:**
> "This mirrors what I've built with n8n + Claude — one blog post becomes 5 content pieces automatically. The part most people miss: you still need a human quality gate between draft and publish. What's your threshold for when AI output is 'good enough' to publish?"

**On an Andrej Karpathy technical post:**
> "This is the clearest explanation of [concept] I've seen. We're using this exact pattern in production with Claude Code for agentic coding workflows — the context window management is the key bottleneck. Curious if you see retrieval-augmented approaches or longer context windows as the better path forward?"

**On a Pascal Bornet enterprise automation post:**
> "Coming from EY consulting, this resonates. The gap isn't technology — it's change management. I've seen automation projects with 10x ROI potential stall because middle management wasn't brought along. What's the most effective internal champion model you've seen?"

## The Collaboration Ladder

```
Level 1: They see your comments (Week 1-2)
Level 2: They like/reply to your comments (Week 2-4)
Level 3: They visit your profile (Week 3-6)
Level 4: They engage with YOUR posts (Month 2-3)
Level 5: DM conversation (Month 2-3)
Level 6: Collaboration (Month 3-6)
```

### Collaboration Ideas by Creator Type

**For Builders (Greg Isenberg, Ruben Hassid):**
- Co-build an automation workflow live
- Template swap (your Notion template, their tool/resource)
- "I built [thing] using your advice" case study

**For Educators (Andrew Ng, Ethan Mollick, Andrej Karpathy):**
- Cite their research in your blog posts
- Create a "simplified for practitioners" version of their technical content
- Reference in your Foundations of AI newsletter

**For Enterprise Voices (Pascal Bornet, Bernard Marr, Allie K. Miller):**
- Case study: "How I applied [their framework] in a real consulting engagement"
- Contrasting perspectives post
- Guest contribution to their newsletter

## Key Metrics to Track

- **Comments per week** on Dream 30 posts (target: 15-25)
- **Reply rate** — what % of your comments get responses
- **Profile views** from Dream 30 engagement
- **New followers** attributable to commenting strategy
- **DM conversations** initiated and progressed
- **Collaborations** pitched and accepted
