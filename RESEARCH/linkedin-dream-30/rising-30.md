## Engagement Strategy for Smaller Creators

Unlike the Dream 30, these creators will **actually notice and engage back**. Prioritise:

1. **Comment first, DM second.** Be a regular face in their comments for 2 weeks before DM.
2. **Share their content.** Repost with your own perspective — they'll see the notification.
3. **Collaborate early.** Smaller creators are hungry for collabs. Pitch within 30 days.
4. **Build a pod.** Find 5-8 from this list at similar stages — form an informal engagement group.
5. **Cross-promote templates/resources.** Especially with Notion and automation builders.
